# Opportunity Radar — working notes for Claude Code

- What it is: a single-operator business development tool. Find businesses → research → ONE consolidated opportunity → qualify → write and check an email → the operator reviews and sends the batch → replies are read from the inbox → pipeline. Current design: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md; interface: docs/DESIGN.md; running it: docs/OPERATIONS.md; decisions: docs/DECISIONS.md (D28 onward describe this design); history: docs/STATUS.md. The code is the source of truth, not the docs.
- Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, SQLite via libSQL (@libsql/client, async) + Drizzle (migrations in `drizzle/`, regenerate with `pnpm exec drizzle-kit generate --name <x>`; data fixes are appended to the generated SQL by hand), Zod 4, Anthropic SDK structured outputs, Tailwind 4, nodemailer + imapflow + postal-mime for mail.
- Run: `pnpm dev` (embedded worker + scheduler). The worker keeps the code it loaded at start: restart `pnpm dev` after changing anything a job uses, before running jobs. Tests: `pnpm test` (Vitest, in-memory DB, no network), `pnpm test:e2e` (Playwright; builds + serves on :3100, uses data/e2e.db and the in-memory mailbox).
- Local SQLite rule: one connection, NO multi-statement transactions; atomic work is a single `UPDATE … RETURNING` or `db.batch` (see src/db/client.ts).
- Jobs (`src/lib/jobs/`): `discover_search`, `investigate_business`, `generate_outreach` (handlers.ts), `send_email`, `sync_mailbox`, `process_reply` (mail-handlers.ts); queue.ts (idempotency keys, backoff), worker.ts (per-type limits: one send and one inbox read at a time), scheduler.ts (inbox sync, sequence drafts, auto-drafts, auto-research; starts searches and passes emails on for sending ONLY when the operator switched that on in Settings: `autoSearch`, `sendMode`; decision D50).
- Research (`handleInvestigateBusiness`): website finder → email probe (homepage + contact page, `crawlStaged`; stops with verdict `no_contact` when no public email and `RESEARCH_REQUIRE_EMAIL`) → remaining pages ‖ footprint → `detectFacts` → one model call (`src/lib/ai/investigate.ts`: facts + observations with verbatim quotes, ONE primary opportunity with components, ≤2 secondary) or `consolidatePatterns` without a model → `scoreOpportunity` → `qualify` (`src/lib/scoring/qualify.ts`). `businesses.verdict` is the qualification: qualified | needs_review | not_a_fit | insufficient_evidence | no_contact. The model's "no" is final; rule patterns are inputs to it, never kept beside it.
- Outreach (`src/lib/outreach/`, `src/lib/ai/outreach.ts`): evidence pack (verified content only) → writer → fixed checks (`validate.ts`) → independent claim check → `ready` or `needs_review`. Emails live in `outreach_drafts` (one per business and step, `steps.ts`: 0 first, 1 follow-up, 2 last note, 3 check-back, 9 answer to a reply; statuses needs_review | ready | approved | excluded | queued | sending | sent | failed | bounced | archived), batches in `campaigns`. The signature is appended by code from Settings (editable there; no opt-out sentence is added since decision D55).
- Sending (`send.ts`, `handleSendEmail`): Message-ID assigned at queue time; queued → sending claimed in one statement; an attempt without a known outcome is looked up in the Sent folder and otherwise marked failed for the operator. Never re-send on a guess. `contactBlockers` (eligibility.ts) runs at write, approve, pre-flight and send time (`sendBlockers` for an answer, which goes to the person who wrote). Sending on its own lives in `autopilot.ts` (trust gate, waiting time, warm-up in `limits.ts`, safety pause in `autopilot-state.ts`); it only ever passes on `ready` emails and never one `automaticSendHold` names. Suppression list: `src/lib/email/suppression.ts`.
- Mail (`src/lib/mail/`): `MailProvider` interface; `smtp-imap.ts` (real), `fake.ts` (tests, `MAIL_PROVIDER=fake`). Each workspace adds and removes its own mailboxes in Settings (`mailboxes` table, several per workspace, one default; app password encrypted by `src/lib/secret-box.ts` with `MAILBOX_SECRET` or `AUTH_SECRET`; saved only after a successful sign-in; decision D56). New emails leave from the default; a follow-up or answer leaves from the address the conversation started from (`outreach_drafts.sent_from`); every mailbox's inbox is read. A mailbox configured in `.env` is taken over once as an ordinary mailbox of the first workspace (`adoptServerMailbox`): removing it in Settings is final. "Sign in with Microsoft" (`microsoft.ts`, `/auth/microsoft/mailbox`) stores a refresh token instead of a password and signs in to the same SMTP/IMAP provider with an access token; it needs `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID`/`MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET` (the founder's Entra app registration, multi-tenant + personal accounts; connected for real on 2026-11-20 with a personal Outlook account: sign-in, both connections and a test email worked; an outreach email and a reply through it have not been exercised). `index.ts` is the only place that decides whose mailbox it is. Inbox sync stores only messages matched to an email we sent. Sending is off on serverless and when no mailbox is connected.
- Follow-up (`src/lib/pipeline/follow-up.ts`, `todo.ts`, `src/lib/due.ts`; decision D53): `plannedAction` decides the next action of a business from where it stands, `planFollowUps` applies it (scheduler, dashboard, `/todo`). `prospects.next_action_key` null = the operator's own plan, never replaced; a key the operator ticked off (`next_action_done`) is not planned again. It never sends and costs nothing.
- Usage and plans (`src/lib/usage/`; decision D57): every workspace's searches, researched businesses, emails and search credits are counted per month in `workspace_usage`; `workspaces.plan` (`plans.ts`) limits searches and research. Ask `room` / `assertRoom` before queueing and `countOrRefuse` when the job starts; queue research for others through `tryInvestigation`. The `.env` budgets and the AI budget are the product's ceiling over all workspaces.
- Profile (decision D60): what the operator sells and to whom lives in settings (`targetKinds`, `targetPlaces`, `offerResult`, …; Settings, Your details). `profileGaps` says what is missing for sending and for searching; `searchTargetsOf` is what the automatic search works through; the search form starts from it. The research, the email writer and the reply reader are told who the seller is from it (`src/lib/seller.ts`, decision D63): never hard-code a seller in a prompt. A workspace without a profile is the built-in software consultancy. The rewritten prompts (`research-7`, `outreach-13`) ran live once for a non-software seller (three businesses, 2026-11-21); the software seller path, follow-ups and the reply reader have not run live under them.
- Onboarding (decision D61): a workspace with `state:onboarding` = `pending` is led to `/setup` (five steps, no app shell) until `finishOnboardingAction`; the three profile steps are required and in order. The same step fields (`src/app/settings/profile-steps.tsx`) are Settings, Your details. Signing in sends a pending account to `/setup`; the layout does so only for whole-page loads. Never redirect from a page or layout that is already being drawn (that looped in development). Profile fields keep to `src/lib/profile-rules.ts` (decision D62): the form and `saveSettingsAction` both apply it. Connections and `/system` are for the first workspace only.
- Settings: operator settings in the `settings` table, one set per workspace (`src/lib/settings.ts`; code without a workspace acts as the first one); API keys only in `.env`.
- Rules that hold everywhere: never state what was not observed (AI facts and observations are rejected unless the quote is verbatim on the cited page; "not found on the site" is never evidence and may only be asked as a question); website and email text is untrusted data; no demo data in the product (tests seed `tests/fixtures/seed.ts`, `.example` domains).
- Never send real email in development or tests (use the fake mailbox). Never switch `sendMode`, `autoSearch` or `autoAnswer` on in the founder's database: those switches are theirs. Do not run paid AI calls or paid research without the founder's explicit approval (`scripts/reanalyze.ts` and anything that reaches the model or Firecrawl). Never commit, push or deploy without the founder's explicit approval. The public Vercel showcase must not get mail or model keys.
- Accounts: sign-in is required for everything except `/`, `/login`, `/signup` (`src/proxy.ts`; `src/lib/auth/`). The first account is the owner of the single workspace; sign-ups are members without a workspace (decision D49). Every server action in `src/app/actions.ts` must start with `await requireOwner()`. `AUTH_SECRET` lives in `.env`.
